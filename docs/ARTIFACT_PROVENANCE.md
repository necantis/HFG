## 3. Provenance & Privacy Governance Rules

1. **Canonical vs. Task-Local Artifact Scope (`ADR-0007`)**: Implementation branches produce task-local artifact descriptors; only the **integration process** publishes canonical production registrations in `runs/<run_id>/manifest.json` after verifying merged code commits and SHA-256 hashes.
2. **Orthogonality in Preference Provenance (`ADR-0005` & `ADR-0008`)**: No downstream artifact may collapse `semantic_importance`, `coder_confidence`, `coder_agreement`, or missingness flags into choice probabilities (`alternative_probabilities`).
3. **Three-Way Counterfactual Provenance Boundary (`ADR-0009`)**: Every preference, structural deduction, or sensitivity assumption entering `FRONTIER_B` MUST carry one mutually exclusive provenance tag:
   - **`OBSERVED_RESPONDENT_PREFERENCE`**: Empirical distributions `P_i = {p_A, p_B}` and missingness states from `PreferenceMatrix` (never modified or re-weighted by counterfactual models).
   - **`INSTITUTIONALLY_DERIVED_MECHANISM`**: Deterministic logical/accounting consequences entailed by a regime definition (e.g., under `CLUB_GOOD`, `non_member_compulsory_contribution = 0.0` removes compulsory cross-subsidy burden from non-members; `non_member_access = NONE` excludes non-members from club usage). **Never** fabricates endorsement of club provision.
   - **`COUNTERFACTUAL_PREFERENCE_ASSUMPTION`**: Explicit, named behavioral/utility sensitivity assumptions over unasked regime features, restricted exclusively to labeled `FRONTIER_B` sweeps and never converted silently from refusal-to-pay spans (*"I do not want compulsory payment"* ≠ *"I prefer club provision"*).
4. **Zero LLM Re-Coding & Sink-Only Reporting**: Under `ADR-0009`, **`NEW_LLM_CODING_REQUIRED = NO`**. Presentation artifacts (`runs/<run_id>/reports/*.md`) cannot feed back into analytical computation or manifest hash inputs for upstream stages.
